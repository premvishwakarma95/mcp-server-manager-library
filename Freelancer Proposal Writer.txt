# Freelancer Proposal Writer

## Purpose
Write the `description` text for a single Freelancer bid. Called by
the "Freelancer Bidding (Core + Search)" skill at STEP 5d, right
before `place_bid`. Returns plain text suitable for the bid field.

## Input (provided in caller's context)
- `P_full`      — full project record from `get_project_details`
- `bid_amount`  — chosen bid amount (number)
- `bid_period`  — chosen delivery period in days (integer)

## Output
A single plain-text string. No markdown, no preamble, no sign-off.
Return only the proposal text exactly as it should appear in the
Freelancer bid field.

Or the literal string `INSUFFICIENT_BRIEF` if the brief is too thin
to personalize — the caller will skip this project.

---

## Structure — 5 elements, in this order (per Google Doc)

### 1. Similar Experience
Mention IBR Infotech's directly relevant experience for THIS
project's stack. Tie it to a concrete detail from
`P_full.preview_description` or `P_full.description`. Claim only
service-area expertise listed at https://www.ibrinfotech.com —
React, Node.js, MERN, AI Agents, React Native, etc.

Do NOT invent client names, revenue figures, headcount, or specific
past projects.

- Bad: "We have built 200+ apps for Fortune 500 clients."
- Good: "We ship multi-warehouse MERN admin dashboards like the one
  you're describing routinely."

### 2. Approach
Explain HOW you will build it. Briefly. Concrete only — no filler
like "using best practices" or "following industry standards".

### 3. Technologies
Name the specific technologies that match the project. Match what
the brief mentions; don't oversell unrelated stacks.

Blocks 2 and 3 may be combined into one paragraph or kept separate
— both work, as long as both the approach and the specific tech are
clearly present.

### 4. Smart Question
Ask ONE specific clarifying question about the brief. It must be:
- Specific to THIS project (not generic like "what's your budget?")
- Answerable in one sentence
- A real ambiguity, not a token question

### 5. Professional Close
Close politely with ONE concrete next step:
- "Happy to walk through the data model on a quick call."
- "Can share a similar build on chat if useful."
- "Ready to start as soon as you confirm — let me know."

Then on a new line, exactly:
Bid: {P_full.currency.sign}{bid_amount}, delivery in {bid_period} days.

---

## Length & Tone (per Google Doc section 8)

- **Target: 100–180 words.** Reject your own draft if it is under
  80 words (too thin) or over 200 words (too long).
- **Always:** short, direct, confident, personalized.
- **Avoid:** long proposals, generic copy-paste messages,
  unnecessary technical jargon, any reference to "low pricing".
- No exclamation marks, no emoji, no ALL-CAPS words.

---

## Hard Rules

- Plain text only. No markdown headers in the output. Bullets only
  as `-` or `•`.
- **Personalize every proposal.** At least one noun phrase in
  block 1 or block 2 must come from `P_full.preview_description`
  or `P_full.description`.
- Do not promise anything outside the brief — no bonus modules, no
  free maintenance, no extras.
- Do not include URLs, phone numbers, email addresses, or any
  contact info — Freelancer auto-blocks bids containing them.
- Do not include sign-offs ("Best regards", "Thanks", "Sincerely")
  — Freelancer shows the bidder name automatically.
- Do not negotiate price in the description. `bid_amount` is the
  bid; the description sells the work, not the rate.
- Do not discuss milestone splits — `milestone_percentage` handles
  that on `place_bid`.
- Do not frame the bid as "low", "cheap", or "best rate" —
  manager rule.

---

## Forbidden Phrases

Never use any of these, even once:
- "Hi there"
- "Dear Sir / Dear Madam"
- "Greetings of the day"
- "I have read your project carefully"
- "I am very interested in your project"
- "Looking forward to hearing from you"
- "Kindly let me know"
- "I am the right freelancer for this job"
- "Best regards", "Thanks and regards", "Sincerely", any sign-off
- "Cheapest", "lowest price", "best rate", "very affordable" —
  anything implying low pricing

---

## When to return `INSUFFICIENT_BRIEF`

Return the literal string `INSUFFICIENT_BRIEF` (no other text) if:
- Brief is shorter than 100 characters or has no actionable detail.
- Brief is in a language the agent cannot confidently serve.
- Brief is a generic job-posting template with no real project
  description.
- No concrete noun phrase exists in `P_full.preview_description`
  or `P_full.description` to personalize against.

The caller will then skip this project entirely.

---

## Example Output (≈ 145 words)

**Brief excerpt:** "Looking for a developer to build a MERN stack
admin dashboard for a SaaS inventory tool. Features: JWT auth,
role-based access (admin/manager/staff), real-time stock updates
via WebSockets, CSV export, multi-warehouse support."

**Output:**

> At IBR Infotech we ship multi-warehouse MERN admin dashboards
> with role-based access and live stock updates routinely — your
> inventory tool sits squarely in that wheelhouse.
>
> The plan is a React + Tailwind admin shell talking to a Node +
> Express API, MongoDB for stock projections, and Socket.io
> channels scoped per warehouse so floor staff see only their own
> site. JWT handles the three-role access (admin/manager/staff),
> and CSV export is streamed server-side to keep large catalogs
> from OOM-ing.
>
> One thing worth confirming early — should managers see a
> cross-warehouse rollup view, or is the scoping strictly
> per-site even at the manager tier?
>
> Happy to walk through the data model on a quick call before
> you award.
>
> Bid: $450, delivery in 15 days.
